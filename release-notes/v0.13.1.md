## Inventory fixes

- **Rings and bracelets go on either hand or wrist.** With one already on, double-clicking a second ring or bracelet
  now puts it on the free hand or wrist, the left one included, and dropping one on a paperdoll square puts it on that
  one. Before, a second one never went on.
- **Shirts and pants stay on together.** Putting on a shirt no longer takes your pants off (or the other way round), and
  the shirt no longer shows in the Pants square.
- **Drag items onto a vendor to sell them.** An item dragged from the Inventory window and let go over the game's vendor
  window goes on the vendor's sell list, as it does from the game's own pack, with the green ring while you hold it
  there. Before, it was dropped on the ground.
- **Items you buy show up straight away.** An item bought from a vendor's own stock of unique items (ones sold back to it,
  and market listings) used to be missing from the Inventory window until you logged in again. This fix needs the
  matching server update.
