## Loot rules

- **Sellable only.** Tick it on a rule, next to **Salvageable only**, and the rule skips anything a Market Broker won't
  list: attuned, character-bound and unstable items, and kinds the market doesn't take (keys, say). What it skips falls
  to the rules after it. This needs the Daralet server's latest update; until then, every item counts as sellable.
- **Gems means gems.** A Misc rule's **Type: Gems** now only takes gems with a material. Things the game counts as gems
  but aren't (no material) count as **Other** instead. The Market tab's search is unchanged.
- **A $ mark.** Rules can mark what they decide with a **Dollar sign**, alongside the star, diamond, dot, triangle and
  exclamation mark.
- **Confirm loot and salvage to start.** The looter starts in **Confirm loot and salvage** now, so the Corpse window
  lists what your rules matched and you choose what to loot. If you've used the looter before, it keeps the mode you
  picked.

## A new Example Loot Profile

- **Rebuilt from the ground up,** in folders by kind of item: melee and thrown, missile and caster weapons, heavy,
  light and cloth armor, amulets, bracelets and rings, clothing, sigil trinkets, transfer spells, misc and value.
- **Marked by how well each item rolled:** gold for the best, then purple, blue and green. A star means the item's
  other mods rolled well too.
- **Value rules** keep sellable items worth a lot for their burden, marked with the new $.
- At the end it salvages what's left (you need an Ust) and ignores anything worth under 100.
- New players get it to start. Everyone else: press **Example** in the Loot tab. It's added next to your old one, which
  stays as it was, and put in use.

## Loot highlights

- **The Inventory's "Loot marks" box is now "Loot highlights".** It works the same: your loot rules' highlights and
  marks on your items. It stays on if you had it on.

## Fixes

- **Item details that never came.** An item's tooltip or the Appraisal window could say "Getting the details..."
  forever if the server didn't answer (when it's busy) or wouldn't describe the item (a bank item after the bank
  closed). The plugin asks again now, and says why when it can't: the server only describes what you carry or wear,
  and the bank's items while it's open. A bank item's appraisal fills in once you open the bank again.
