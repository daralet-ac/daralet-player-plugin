## Toolbar buttons

- **The game's toolbar can open the plugin's windows.** Tick **Toolbar button** under the Inventory, Map, Quests or
  Character window's settings, and the game toolbar's backpack, Map (compass) or Contracts (quill) button, or the
  Character Information pack beside it, opens the plugin's window instead of the game's panel. Each is on or off by
  itself, and all start off.
- The plugin draws its own copy of each button over the game's, lit while its window is open, as the game lights a
  panel's button. The Character Information pack goes yellow when you're overburdened and red when you're very
  overburdened.
- Drop an item from the plugin's lists on the backpack button and it goes in your main pack.
- The game's own keys for those panels still open the game's panels.

## New windows

- **Quests window:** the Quests tab in a window of its own, to keep on screen while you play. It shares the tab's search
  and picked quest. Turn it on under the Quests tab's **Settings**.
- **Character window:** the Character tab in a window of its own. Turn it on under the Character tab's **Settings**.
- **The Character tab starts with your Character Information,** in the game's own words: age, deaths, birth, innate
  attributes, natural resistances and regeneration, augmentations, luminance augmentations and burden, followed by the
  character stats it already showed.

## Fixes

- **A crash during login.** The plugin could crash the game at character select or while logging in, when a window (the
  Map, say) read where you were before the game knew. Nothing reads your position, heading, selection or chat bar until
  you're logged in now, and the plugin's floating windows stay hidden until then.
