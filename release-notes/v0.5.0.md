## Installing is now one download

**Daralet-Setup-0.5.0.exe** installs Daralet and adds it to Decal for you, ticked. There's no more Add, Browse in the
Decal agent.

- It checks that you have Decal 2.9.8 and .NET 4.8, and says where to get them if you don't.
- It installs **UtilityBelt.Service** if it's missing (tick the box it shows). Without it, Daralet can't open its window.
- If you added `Daralet.dll` to Decal by hand, it replaces that copy in the same folder. If you're coming from v0.1.0,
  it also removes the old `DaraletPlayer.dll`.
- Close the game and the Decal agent before running it; it asks you to if they're open.
- To update to a later version, run that version's installer. Your settings and loot profiles are kept.
- To uninstall, use Windows' **Installed apps** (Add or remove programs). It takes Daralet out of Decal and asks whether
  to delete your settings too.

You can still install by hand: `Daralet.dll` is attached as before. See "Installing by hand" in the README.

## Inventory window, the way the game lays it out

The pop-out Inventory window has a new **Retail** layout, on by default: your paperdoll and packs side by side at the
top, and the chosen pack's items in a grid across the whole width below. Untick Retail for the old view.

- **The game's own pictures:** the main pack, empty slots, the selection frame and the pack backgrounds come from the
  client.
- **Pack column:** each pack has a bar down its side showing how full it is, and empty squares show the side pack slots
  you have left. A burden bar shows how much you're carrying; hover over it for the exact amount. It turns yellow past
  100% and is full at 300%, the most you can carry.
- **Reset width** makes the window just wide enough for the paperdoll and the packs beside it, as the game's inventory
  is. In the plain view it fits six columns of items.
- **Grids** show every slot in a pack, empty ones too, with no gaps between them, like the game's. Clicking an item
  selects it.

## Drag and drop

- **Drag packs onto each other** to change their order, in any pack list, yours or the bank's.
- **Every drop is checked first.** A green ring shows where the item can go, and a red circle where it can't: a pack
  inside a pack, a full pack, or the wrong paperdoll square. Letting go over a red circle does nothing.
- Dropping on a pack puts the item at the front; dropping on an empty slot puts it at the end.
- **Drag an item out of the plugin** onto a person, NPC or creature to give it to them, or onto the ground to drop it.
- **Drag something from the ground** into one of the plugin's packs or slots to pick it up.
- A dragged item shows only its own icon, as in the game.
- Lists update the moment the game moves an item, instead of up to half a second later.

## Loot rules

- **Weapon subtype:** Melee / Thrown, Missile or Caster. Picking one shows only the filters (and weapon classes) that go
  with it.
- **New filters:** a launcher's damage mod, a caster's elemental damage and restoration, **Damage element** (pick any
  number of elements), and burden and value per burden for every category.
- **Each filter's tooltip** shows the range loot rolls that stat in, at the rule's tiers, so you know what numbers make
  sense to enter.
- The rule list shows each rule's mark, and the outcome buttons are coloured to match.
- **Item tooltips** end with the rule that decides the item, in its outcome's colour.
