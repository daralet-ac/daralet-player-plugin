## Dungeon map

- **A map of the dungeon you're in.** Turn on **Dungeon map** in the World tab, and the Dungeon window maps the
  dungeon around you, drawn from the game's own files, so it works in every dungeon, Daralet's new ones included. It
  needs nothing from the server.
- **Floors.** Your floor is bright, the four below it darker the deeper they are, and the one above faint. Ramps have
  their own colour. Tick **All floors** to see every floor at once.
- **Follows you.** **Follow me** keeps you in the middle (dragging the map turns it off), and **Turn with me** turns the
  map as you turn, with a needle pointing north. Zoom with the mouse wheel.
- **Rooms you've been in** are greyed out, or drawn in a colour you pick under **Show...**. They're remembered for each
  character and each dungeon; **Forget** clears a dungeon.
- **Markers** for portals, your corpse, other corpses, chests, NPCs and monsters. Only what's on your floor, in your
  room or rooms you've been in, shows: the map shows what you've seen, not everything nearby. Show or hide each kind
  under **Show...**, and click one to select it.
- **The way.** **Way out** draws the shortest way to the nearest portal you've seen. Right-click a marker or a spot for
  the way there; the way to a monster follows it as it moves.
- **Only when needed** shows the window only while you're indoors. Its settings are under the World tab's
  **Settings**.

## Settings profiles

- **A new Config tab,** first in the window. Every setting the plugin keeps (its windows and where they are, every tab's
  options, your loot profiles and hotbars) is kept in a profile, and each character picks its own, so your main and your
  mule can each have their own set up, or share one.
- **New, Copy, Rename and Delete.** New starts a profile with every setting as it starts; Copy starts one from the one
  you're using. The character you're on switches to it, and its windows move to where that profile keeps them.
- **To file and From file** save a profile as a file, to keep it or give it to a friend, and add one from a file.
  **Open folder** shows where they go. The rooms you've been in stay yours and aren't saved in the file.
- **Your settings are where they were:** everything you had is the **Default** profile, which every character uses
  until you pick another.

## Fixes

- **A held wand on a hotbar.** Pressing a hotbar slot for a wand, staff or orb you were already holding brought up the
  targeting reticle, or tried to use the wand on the monster you had selected. Now a slot set to **Auto** does nothing
  for a caster you're holding. Set to **Yourself**, **Your selection** or **Pick**, it still does what it's set to.
