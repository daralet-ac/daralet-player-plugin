## Hotbars

The Target tab is now **Combat**, with two sub tabs: **Target**, as before, and **Hotbars**: bars of items and spells you
use with a click or a key, each in a window of its own. Tick **Shown** next to a bar in Combat → Hotbars to put it on
screen; the first one starts hidden.

- **Putting things on a bar:** drag an item from the Inventory tab, the Inventory window or the game's own pack, or a
  spell from the game's spellbook or spell bar. While you hold it over a slot, its icon shows there faintly, so you can
  see where it'll go. You can also right-click a slot and pick **Put a spell here** to find a spell by name.
- **Using a slot:** click it, or press its hotkey. Gear goes on or comes off, potions and other items are used, and spells
  are cast (in spellcasting mode only: out of it, the bar tells you).
- **Healing kits:** with **One-click Heal** (on by default), a healing, stamina or mana kit goes straight onto you. Set
  a kit's slot to **Your selection** in its right-click menu (Use on) to heal someone else with it.
- **Running out:** when a stack runs out, the slot moves on to the next one like it you carry. With none left, it shows
  shaded until you pick more up.
- **Each character** has their own items on the bars; a bar's layout, look and keys are shared.

### What the slots show

- **Cooldowns**, straight from the server: potions, kits, combat abilities, and the ones a Combat Focus starts. Items that
  share a cooldown all show it at once. Pick how: a clock sweep, a fill, a dim, or a bar, with the seconds left in the
  middle and a flash when it's ready.
- **Uses left** on kits, lockpicks and mana stones, as a bar along one side of the slot.
- **How many you carry** in the corner, and a frame round what you're wearing.
- **Hotkeys** in a corner, sharp and bright at any size.

### Arranging

- Slots don't move by accident. Turn on **Move slots** to drag them to another place, on any bar, or off the bar to
  empty it. The rest of the time, right-click a slot for **Move...** and **Move to**.
- **Bind keys:** turn it on, hover a slot and press a key (with Ctrl, Shift or Alt if you like). Or use the Keys tab's
  quick sets: 1 to 0, Shift + 1 to 0, or F1 to F12 across a row. The game still hears the keys too, so pick ones it
  doesn't use.

### Make it yours

Each bar has its own settings, with a preview that shows your changes as you make them:

- **Layout:** up to 24 slots across, 12 rows and 10 pages, slot size and spacing, page arrows and mouse-wheel paging.
- **Look:** four ready-made styles (Classic, Dark, Minimal, Gold), or every colour picked by hand, plus text sizes, the
  border and the background.
- **Cooldowns and uses:** the cooldown style, the size of its numbers and whether they show tenths, and the uses bar's
  side and thickness.
- **Keys:** each slot's key, page keys, and where and how big the keys show.

## Also

- **Dragging from the game's pack onto a plugin window no longer drops the item on the ground.** If the game drops it
  anyway, Daralet picks it straight back up.
- **Locking a window** (the target, fellowship and inventory windows too) now keeps what's in it exactly where it was,
  instead of it jumping up.
- Every window's **Size** slider has a **Reset** button that puts it back to 100%.

settings and loot profiles are kept.
