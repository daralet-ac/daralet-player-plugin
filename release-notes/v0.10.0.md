## Find a group

- **The Fellowship tab has two parts now:** **Members**, which is what the tab showed before, and **Find a group**. When
  you aren't in a fellowship, Members has a **Find a group** button that takes you there.
- **Look for a group, or for more players.** Pick what for: a capstone (one by name, or any, up to a level, at a
  difficulty), leveling, quests (in a town, or anywhere), or anything else in a few words. Then press **Look for a
  group**, or, as a fellowship's leader, **Look for more**. Tick **Ask me first** to be asked yes or no about each
  player, instead of them joining at once.
  - Look for a group, and you join the first fellowship that's looking for more for the same thing, as soon as there
    is one. Leveling only puts you with a fellowship that would share XP evenly with you.
  - Look for more, and players looking for it join your fellowship until it's full.
- **See who's looking.** Find a group lists the fellowships looking for more and the players looking for a group, in
  green where they fit you. **Join** a fellowship (or **Ask**, if its leader asks about each player), or **Invite** a
  player to yours.
- **The form shows the command it sends**, so anyone can do the same without the plugin. The commands are
  `/lfgroup` (looking for a group: `/lfgroup gmg d10`, `/lfgroup join <name>`, `/lfgroup list`, `/lfgroup stop`) and
  `/lfmore` (looking for more: `/lfmore gmg d20`, `/lfmore invite <name>`, `/lfmore list`, `/lfmore stop`). Type
  `/lfgroup` on its own for everything they take.
- These need the Daralet server's latest update. Until then, Find a group says the server is too old.

## LFG chat

- Each time someone looks for a group or for more, the server posts it in the LFG channel, with what to type to answer
  it.
- **Join and Invite links.** With the plugin, those lines get a **[Join]** or **[Invite]** link on the end: click it to
  join that fellowship, or invite that player to yours. Only you see the links. Turn them off under Find a group's
  **Settings**.
