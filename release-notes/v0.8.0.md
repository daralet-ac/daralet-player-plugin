## Loot rules, organised

- **Folders.** Group rules into folders: Ctrl+click or Shift+click to pick several rules and press **Put in a folder**,
  press **Add folder** for an empty one at the top, or right-click a rule for **New folder**, **Move to folder** and
  **Take out of folder**. Folders open and shut, have a checkbox that turns all their rules on or off, and rename,
  ungroup or duplicate from their right-click menu. A folder's rules stay together, so the list is still checked top to
  bottom.
- **Drag to reorder.** Drag a rule, every picked rule, or a whole folder. Dropped on a rule, it goes just above it, in
  that rule's folder; on a folder, at the end of that folder; below the list, at the very end. Hold a drag near the top
  or bottom of the list to scroll it.
- **Duplicate** a rule, the picked rules, or a folder and all its rules from the right-click menu.
- **A wider rule list.** Drag the bar between the list and the rule's settings to widen it (double-click the bar for the
  usual width). A rule's mark now always sits at the right end of its row, with a long name cut short before it.

## Filters that fit the item

- **Weapon Type.** Pick Sword, Axe, Atlatl, Shuriken, Orb, Wand, Scepter and more, and only that weapon's filters show,
  including its special property: **Biting Strike**, **Crushing Blow**, **Armor Cleaving**, **Ward Cleaving** or
  **Stamina Cost Reduction**. Weapon types need the Daralet server's latest update; until then a rule with a type picked
  matches nothing.
- **Armor weight** (cloth, light, heavy) shows only the mods that weight rolls, and **jewelry slot** (amulet, bracelet,
  ring) only the two ratings that slot rolls.
- **Headings.** A rule's filters sit under Types, Damage, Protection, Mods, Magic and Misc.
- **OR for mods.** Tick **OR** by the Mods heading and an item needs only one of the mods you filled in, while the
  rule's other filters still all have to hold.
- **Max speed** replaces Min speed: it's the weapon's own Speed number, as its tooltip shows it, where lower is faster.
  Rules that used the old Min speed need their speed set again.
- **Min proc chance %** for weapons, under Magic.
- **Several spells.** The Spell and Scroll spell boxes take several names separated by commas, and an item with any one
  of them matches: `swift hunter, blood drinker`.

## Corpse window

- **Loot** is now **Loot keeps**: it loots only the ticked items your rules **Keep**, and leaves the ones they
  **Salvage** in the corpse. **Loot and salvage** loots both, then salvages. Each button has its own tooltip, saying
  why it's greyed out when it is.

## Also

- **A new Example Loot Profile:** over a hundred rules in folders for weapons, armor, jewelry, clothing, sigil trinkets,
  scrolls and valuables, marked by how well each item rolled. Your settings keep the example you already have; press
  **Example** on the Loot tab to add the new one.
- The developer loader writes its log to the `Daralet Toolkit` folder, where the plugin's own log is.
